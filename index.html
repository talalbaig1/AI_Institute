AI will not replace your job. Someone using AI will.

Every week brings a new model, a new tool and a new headline. Most people try a chatbot, get an average answer and go back to doing the work by hand.

The gap is not access. It is knowing how to turn AI into a working assistant for your own job, and then a whole team of them.

How we handle your data (PDPL)

What we collect: your name, email address and WhatsApp number, plus the date and wording of your consent.

Why: only to contact you about the Build Your Agent course and its cohorts (dates, venue, fees, registration). We will not use it for anything else without asking you separately, and we do not sell or share it.

Where it is kept: in a private spreadsheet that only the course team can open. It is hosted by Google, so your data may be processed on servers outside Saudi Arabia.

How long: until the cohort you asked about has started, and no longer than 12 months after you register. Then we delete it.

Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time, and we will act within 30 days. Reply STOP to any message we send you and we will stop contacting you and delete your details.

Age: you should be 18 or over to register.
